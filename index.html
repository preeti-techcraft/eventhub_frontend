About Me

Lead Developer & Creator of EventHub

Welcome to EventHub! I am a passionate Full-Stack Developer dedicated to building scalable, beautifully designed web applications. EventHub was born out of a desire to create a seamless, all-in-one platform for organizers to manage events and attendees to securely book their spots.

My technical background spans modern front-end design using Vanilla Javascript, CSS Glassmorphism and responsive architectures, powered by robust Java Spring Boot and MySQL backends. I thrive on solving complex logic problems and delivering a "WOW" experience to end users.
