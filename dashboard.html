Help & Support

Booking Tickets

To book a ticket, navigate to the Overview section and click "Book Spot" on any available event. You can view all your reserved tickets under the My Tickets tab.

Hosting Events

If you are an Organizer, you can publish new events from the Create Event menu. Manage your attendees, view revenue, and upload gallery images from your personalized dashboard.

Contact Support

If you face any issues with ticket booking, event creation, or account management, please reach out to our team at support@eventhub.com or call us at +1 800-123-4567.
