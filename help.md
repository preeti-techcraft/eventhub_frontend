## General Information

### What is EventHub?
EventHub is a platform that bridges the gap between event organizers and attendees. It provides a secure, intuitive interface to create, discover, and book events.

### Managing Your Profile
Every user, regardless of role, can manage their profile settings:
- **Edit Profile:** Update your personal information, such as your bio, profile picture, and contact details from the Dashboard sidebar.
- **Account Settings:** Change your password, update security preferences, or adjust notification settings.

## Attendee Guide (User Role)

### How to Find and Book Events
1. Log in to your EventHub account.
2. Navigate to the **Dashboard**. Here you will see a list of available (approved) events.
3. Click on the **Book Spot** button for an event you wish to attend.
4. If there is available capacity, your booking will be confirmed immediately.

### Viewing Your Bookings
You can view all the events you've successfully booked by going to the **My Bookings** tab on your Dashboard.

## Organizer Guide

### Creating a New Event
1. Log in with your `ORGANIZER` account.
2. Go to your Dashboard and click on **Create Event**.
3. Fill in the event details (Title, Description, Date, Capacity, Pricing, etc.).
4. Submit the form. The event will initially have a `PENDING` status.

### Event Approval Process
Once you create an event, it must be approved by a System Administrator before it becomes visible to attendees. Once approved, its status will change to `APPROVED`.

### Managing Events & Revenue
You can view all the events you have created in your Dashboard. You can monitor the capacity, view attendees, and track your simulated event revenue.

## Administrator Guide

### Approving or Rejecting Events
1. Log in with your `ADMIN` account.
2. The Admin Dashboard will show a list of all events, including those with a `PENDING` status.
3. Review the pending events. You can choose to **Approve** or **Reject** them.
4. Approved events instantly become available for users to book.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### I forgot my password, how do I reset it?
Currently, password resets are handled via the Account Settings modal. If you are entirely locked out, please contact the system administrator.

### Why is my newly created event not showing up for users?
Your event is likely in the `PENDING` state. It needs to be reviewed and approved by an Administrator before it goes live.

### Can I book multiple spots for the same event?
No, the system currently restricts each user to a single booking per event to prevent monopolizing limited-capacity events.

### How does the Payment System work?
Currently, the platform uses a **Simulated Payment System** for testing and demonstration purposes. No real money is charged when booking tickets.

### How do I change my account role?
Account roles are assigned during the sign-up process. If you need to switch from a User to an Organizer, you will need to create a new account or ask an Administrator to update your permissions in the database.
